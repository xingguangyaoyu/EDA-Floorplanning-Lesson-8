    先备知识Before you start

    固定外框Fixed outline

    容器是一个不能移动、不能变大的矩形。这里是 10 列 × 8 行 = 80 格。"合法"永远是相对这个框说的。 The container never moves or grows. Here it is 10 columns × 8 rows = 80 cells. "Legal" is always measured against this frame.

    模块 · 面积 · 标准答案Module, area, golden

    一个模块就是要摆进去的一个矩形，它的面积是 w×h。一个标准答案（golden）是一组已知可行的摆法，用来对照 —— 不是唯一正确的摆法。 A module is one rectangle to place; its area is w·h. A golden is one known-good packing used as a reference — not the only legal answer.

    死区与密度Deadspace and density

    死区 = 外框面积 − 模块面积和；密度 = 模块面积 ÷ 外框面积。这两个数只由面积决定，任何摆法都改不了。 Deadspace = outline area − sum of module areas. Density = module area ÷ outline area. Both come from the areas alone.

    一张图看懂：同样的面积预算，两种摆法One picture: same budget, two floor plans

    • 两种摆法都合法：全在 10×8 内、互不重叠、边可以贴边。 Both packings are legal: inside 10×8, no overlap, edges may touch.
    • 两种摆法的死区都是 57、密度都是 28.75% —— 面积没变，总量就不可能变。 Both have deadspace 57 and density 28.75% — the areas did not change, so the totals cannot.
    • 变的是空格的形状：硬布局在 C 和 E 上方有一块 5 格的空兜；软布局变成第 6 列一条 2 格的缝，加 D 旁边 3 格的条。 What changed is the shape of the empty cells: a 5-cell pocket above C and E becomes a 2-cell gap at column 6 plus a 3-cell strip beside D.

    为什么要用这么小的算例？因为只有小到能用手指头数格子，你才能亲手验证每一句断言 —— 外框 80、面积 23、死区 57、密度 28.75%，这些数都能自己复算。真实芯片上是几百个模块、由搜索算法决定每个软模块的形状，于是你只能相信工具给的数。先把循环在能核对的地方跑通，再去信工具，是更稳的顺序。 Why keep the instance this tiny? Because only at a size you can count by hand can you verify every claim yourself: 80 cells, 23 of area, 57 of deadspace, 28.75% density. On a real chip there are hundreds of blocks and a search algorithm decides each soft shape, so you end up trusting the tool's numbers. Running the loop once where you can check it is the safer order.

      引导练习Guided practice

      任务 1 —— 只有两种形状Task 1 — two shapes only

      面积是 6，在整数网格上把所有因子对列出来，分别算宽除以高。哪几对落在 [0.5, 2] 里？ Area 6: list every factor pair and compute w/h. Which pairs stay inside [0.5, 2]?

      答案：1×6 → 0.167（低于 0.5，非法），2×3 → 0.667（合法），3×2 → 1.5（合法），6×1 → 6.0（高于 2，非法）。合法的正好两种 —— 这就是为什么实验只给两个变形按钮。 Answer: 1×6 → 0.167 (below 0.5, illegal), 2×3 → 0.667 (legal), 3×2 → 1.5 (legal), 6×1 → 6.0 (above 2, illegal). Exactly two survive — hence two reshape buttons.

      任务 2 —— 在纸上做一次偷懒的变形Task 2 — the lazy reshape, on paper

      先写下硬布局的坐标，然后把 A 原地改成 2×3，其他都不动。A 和 D 抢了哪些格子？检查器先报什么？ Write down the hard golden, change A to 2×3 in place, move nothing else. Which cells do A and D both claim? What does the checker say first?

      答案：A 占 x 0–2、y 0–3；D 占 x 0–3、y 2–3。交集 x 0–2、y 2–3 有正面积，所以报 "A overlaps D"。而面积检查（2×3 = 6）是通过的 —— 光有面积守恒，换不来合法。 Answer: A covers x 0–2, y 0–3; D covers x 0–3, y 2–3. The intersection has positive area, so the report is "A overlaps D". The area check (2×3 = 6) passes — area preservation alone never makes a packing legal.

      任务 3 —— 数空格子Task 3 — count the empty cells

      对硬布局，按高度带数空格：y 0–2 两行、y 2–3 一行、y 3–8 五行。加起来是不是 57？ For the hard golden, count empty cells by band: rows y 0–2, row y 2–3, rows y 3–8. Do they sum to 57?

      答案：y 0–2 有 10×2 − 18 = 2 格空（第 9 列）；y 2–3 有 10 − 5 = 5 格空；y 3–8 是 50 格全空。合计 2 + 5 + 50 = 57。软布局同样三段：4 + 3 + 50 = 57 —— 那 5 格空兜拆成第 6 列 2 格的缝，加 D 旁边 3 格的条。 Answer: y 0–2 has 10×2 − 18 = 2 free cells; y 2–3 has 10 − 5 = 5; y 3–8 has 50. Total 2 + 5 + 50 = 57. The soft pack: 4 + 3 + 50 = 57 — the 5-cell pocket split into a 2-cell gap plus a 3-cell strip.

      任务 4 —— 为什么 D 往右走，不往上走Task 4 — why D moves right, not up

      软布局里 D 落在 (4,2)，压在 C 上面。那 D 放到 (2,3)、也就是 B 上方，行不行？ In the soft golden D lands at (4,2) on top of C. Would D at (2,3), above B, also work?

      答案：D 在 (2,3) 是 3×1，占 x 2–5、y 3–4：在框内，也不碰任何模块（A 和 B 都在 y=3 处结束，贴边合法）。所以行。教学标准答案只是选了 (4,2)；任何合法摆法都算数。 Answer: D at (2,3) is 3×1 covering x 2–5, y 3–4: inside, touching nothing (A and B end at y=3; edge contact is legal). So yes. The teaching golden just picks (4,2); any legal packing counts.

      唯一的坑：合法不等于好The one trap: legal is not good

      布图规划里最容易白干的一件事，不是不会摆，而是摆完不知道拿什么判断。合法只是及格线 —— 下面这条才是这课要你带走的判断标准。 The easiest way to waste work in floorplanning is not failing to place blocks; it is not knowing what to judge them by. Legality is only a pass line — this is the judgment the lesson wants you to keep.

      把 legal: true 当成做完了Mistaking legal for good

      症状：legal 显示 true，你收工了，可外接矩形还是 10×8，模块散得到处都是。 Symptom: legal shows true and you stop, but the bounding box is still 10×8 with modules scattered everywhere.

      原因：合法只查两件事 —— 每个模块在框内、彼此不重叠。五块散着放全都满足，但它不是一个好布局：走线会长，变形带来的那点空隙全白费。 Cause: legality checks exactly two things — inside the frame, no overlap. Scattered modules satisfy both, yet the floorplan is bad: wires get long and the reshape bought nothing.

      修法：摆完把外接矩形和 legal 一起读。散放是 10×8 = 80，紧凑能到 6×4 = 24 —— 这就是挑战 2 要你压到 24 的原因。 Fix: read the bounding box next to legal. Scattered is 10×8 = 80; tight reaches 6×4 = 24 — exactly what Challenge 2 asks for.

      在实验里怎么看到它：每摆一步，指标面板最后一行会报 bbox。散着放的时候它是 10×8 = 80；只有当你把空隙挤到只剩一格，它才会变成 6×4 = 24。数字不会骗人，现象一眼可见。 How to see it in the lab: the last metrics line reports the bbox on every placement. Scattered sits at 10×8 = 80; only when the gaps are squeezed down to a single cell does it read 6×4 = 24.

      术语表Glossary

      outline 外框
      固定的容器矩形；这里是 10×8 = 80 格。合法性永远相对它来判。The fixed container rectangle; 10×8 = 80 cells here. Legality is measured against it.
      module 模块
      要摆进去的一个矩形。硬模块 w×h 固定，软模块可以变形。One rectangle to place. Hard modules keep a fixed w×h; soft modules may reshape.
      hard module 硬模块
      形状定死、不能变形的模块（SRAM、模拟电路、第三方 IP）—— 厂家给的物理尺寸。A module whose shape is fixed — SRAM, analog circuits, third-party IP. The vendor sets the physical size.
      soft module 软模块
      有形状自由的模块：面积锁死（A 是 w·h = 6），宽高比有界（w/h ∈ [0.5, 2]）。A module with shape freedom: area locked (w·h = 6 for A), aspect bounded (w/h ∈ [0.5, 2]).
      aspect 宽高比
      宽除以高。它是自由的边界，不是摆放的位置属性。Width divided by height. A boundary of the freedom, not a placement property.
      reshape 变形
      在面积＋宽高比约束下为软模块挑一个新的 (w, h)。这是任务的一半。Choosing a new (w, h) under the area + aspect constraints. Half the job.
      re-pack 重摆
      变形之后重新摆硬模块。这是另一半 —— 决定布局合不合法的那一半。Re-placing the hard modules after a reshape. The other half — the one that decides legality.
      place 放置
      把模块的左下角放到某个格 (x, y)。Put a module's lower-left corner at a grid cell (x, y).
      packing 布局
      整个摆法：每个模块都有位置。在框内＋不重叠才算合法。The whole arrangement: every module placed. Legal if inside and non-overlapping.
      legal 合法
      每个模块都在外框内（x≥0, y≥0, x+w≤10, y+h≤8）且没有正面积重叠。贴边合法。Every module inside (x≥0, y≥0, x+w≤10, y+h≤8) and no positive-area overlap. Edge contact is allowed.
      overlap 重叠
      两块模块共享正面积的格子。只是边碰边（面积为零）不算重叠。Two modules sharing cells with positive area. Touching edges does not count.
      deadspace 死区
      外框面积减模块面积：80 − 23 = 57。变形挪得动它，但消不掉它。Outline area minus module area: 80 − 23 = 57. A reshape moves it, never shrinks it.
      density 密度
      模块面积 ÷ 外框面积 = 23/80 = 0.2875（28.75%）。在这个算例里与形状无关。Module area ÷ outline area = 23/80 = 0.2875 (28.75%). Shape-invariant here.
      bounding box 外接矩形
      盖住所有已摆模块的最小矩形。标准答案是 9×3；挑战目标是 24 格。Smallest rectangle covering all placed modules. The golden is 9×3; the challenge target is 24 cells.
      golden 标准答案
      一组已知可行的摆法，用作对照 —— 从来不是唯一合法的答案。One known-good packing kept as a reference — never the only legal answer.

      完整口播稿（逐页）Full narration, slide by slide

      Slide 1 — One module, two shapes

      This is lab eight of floorplanning for EDA: soft module aspect sizing.

      Some blocks keep a fixed width and height; others may change shape as long as their area stays the same.

      Today one module gets reshaped, and the whole packing around it has to answer.

      By the end you will reshape it yourself and judge the result with four numbers.

      Slide 2 — Where this is used

      Before any numbers, here is where this shows up on a real chip.

      Memory blocks and analog circuits are hard: the vendor fixes their shape, so nobody reshapes them.

      Ordinary logic is soft: its gate count fixes the area, and its standard cell rows let the shape change.

      Production floorplanners reshape those soft blocks to close the gaps before routing starts.

      A few percent of die area is money on every wafer, and shorter wires mean better timing and less power.

      The real problem has hundreds of blocks, so a search algorithm replaces your hand.

      This lesson keeps that same loop and shrinks it to numbers you can check.

      Slide 3 — The situation: 23 into 80

      Start with the situation.

      The outline is a fixed rectangle, ten by eight, so its area is eighty.

      Five modules must live inside it, and their areas add up to twenty three.

      Four of them are hard modules: width and height never change.

      Module A is the soft one: area locked at six, width over height free to move between one half and two.

      Slide 4 — The method: reshape, then re-pack

      The method is a short loop, and the order matters.

      First choose a shape for A: a width and height whose product is still six, inside the aspect bounds.

      Second, place A into the outline.

      Third, re-pack the four hard modules around the new shape.

      Fourth, judge the result: legal or not, and what did the numbers do.

      Reshaping is only half the job; the re-pack decides whether the layout is legal.

      Slide 5 — The instance: five modules, tiny numbers

      Here is the whole instance, small enough to check by hand.

      The outline is ten columns wide and eight rows tall.

      A starts three wide and two tall, area six, aspect allowed between one half and two.

      B is two by three, C is two by two, D is three by one, and E is two by two.

      The areas are six, six, four, three, and four: twenty three in total.

      Every number today comes from this table, and you can re-derive each one.

      Slide 6 — Baseline: hard A at 3×2

      Baseline first: A keeps the hard shape, three by two, and the golden packing puts it at zero zero.

      B sits at three zero, C at five zero, D at zero two, and E at seven zero.

      Everything stays inside the ten by eight outline, nothing overlaps, so the packing is legal.

      Now the accounting: module areas sum to twenty three, and the outline area is eighty.

      Deadspace is eighty minus twenty three: fifty seven empty cells.

      Density is twenty three over eighty: zero point two eight seven five, about twenty nine percent.

      Keep those two numbers: they follow from the areas alone, not from any one shape.

      Slide 7 — Naive reshape fails: A overlaps D

      Now try the laziest possible reshape and watch it fail.

      Keep A at the same corner, zero zero, but stretch it to two wide and three tall.

      The area check passes: two times three is still six.

      But look at D: it sits at zero two, and A now fills that strip too.

      The checker says it plainly: A overlaps D, illegal.

      A reshape invalidates neighbors, so re-packing is not optional; it is the other half of reshape.

      Slide 8 — Soft golden: same area, new whitespace

      Here is the legal answer: A at zero zero as two by three, and the neighbors re-packed around it.

      B moves to two zero, C to four zero, D to four two, and E stays at seven zero.

      Run the checker again: everyone inside the outline, no overlaps, legal.

      Now the surprise: deadspace is still fifty seven, and density is still twenty eight point seven five percent.

      What changed is the shape of the empty cells: the hard pack has a five cell pocket above C and E.

      The soft pack splits it: a two cell gap at column six, plus a three cell strip beside D.

      Same area budget, different floor plan: that is what aspect freedom buys.

      Slide 9 — Read the panel like a judge

      The lab panel prints five lines, and each line answers a different question.

      Module area is twenty three: the five modules as currently shaped.

      Outline area is eighty and never changes; deadspace is outline area minus module area, here fifty seven.

      Density is module area over outline area: zero point two eight seven five.

      Legal is just true or false.

      The reason names the first violation it finds, like A overlaps D or E outside outline.

      When your numbers disagree with the panel, the reason line is where you look first.

      Slide 10 — Aspect bounds: what softness can and cannot buy

      The aspect bounds are the fence: for A, width over height must stay between one half and two.

      With area six, the integer shapes are one by six, two by three, three by two, and six by one.

      One by six has aspect one sixth; six by one has aspect six.

      Both leave the fence, so both are illegal shapes.

      Only two by three and three by two stay inside, which is why the reshape buttons offer exactly those two.

      And softness cannot lift the density: area over area, no shape of A beats twenty eight point seven five percent.

      Softness rearranges whitespace; it never creates area.

      Slide 11 — The one trap: legal is not good

      One trap is worth this whole page.

      Legal only means two things: every module inside the frame, and no overlap.

      Five modules scattered across the ten by eight still pass that check.

      But then the bounding box is the whole frame, and the reshape bought you nothing.

      So read the bounding box next to legal: legality is a gate, not a score.
